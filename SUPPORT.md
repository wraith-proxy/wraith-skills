# Support

- **Bugs and questions:** open an issue at
  https://github.com/wraith-proxy/wraith-skills/issues. Include your host
  (Claude Code or Copilot CLI) and its version, the wraith version, and
  what you ran.
- **Security problems:** do not open a public issue. See [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **Companion plugins:** report problems to their vendors.

Support is best effort, with no service level.
